research

I'm currently working on improving how machines perceive and explain audio. This involves:
   1. designing better audio representations,
   2. developing better multimodal LLMs, and
   3. devising novel evaluations.


My recent work is primarily in music and bioacoustics.

Previously, I've worked on various topic in music information retrieval, including structure analysis, fingerprinting, representation learning, rearrangement, evaluation, and ethnomusicology.
